# Key Findings: Swiggy Restaurant Analysis

- **Largest restaurant markets:** Bikaner (1,666), Noida-1 (1,427) and Indirapuram, Delhi (1,279) have the most listings. Delhi and Bangalore areas dominate the top 10.
- **Most common cuisines:** "North Indian, Chinese" (6,469) and "Indian" (6,406) lead, followed by Chinese (5,049) and North Indian (4,775).
- **Biggest chains:** Domino's Pizza has the most branches (442), followed by Pizza Hut (319) and KFC (309).
- **Best rated areas (50+ restaurants):** Mylapore, Chennai (4.23), South Kolkata (4.22) and Frazer Town, Bangalore (4.22).
- **Highest rated cuisines (100+ restaurants):** Ice Cream and Desserts (4.36) and Ice Cream (4.32) rate highest.
- **Top restaurants (4.5+ rating, 1000+ ratings):** NIC Natural Ice Creams appears most often in the 4.7-rated group, across Bangalore and Kolkata.
- **Best value for money:** Chennai areas (Mylapore, Adyar) pair top ratings (4.22-4.23) with a moderate average cost of 313-350. Among the top 10 rated areas, Burrabazar, Kolkata is the cheapest (avg cost 291, rating 4.19).
- **Pricing:** Among areas with 50+ restaurants, Khan Market, Delhi is the most expensive (avg cost for two 601), followed by Fort Colaba, Mumbai (493) and North Goa (469). Premium areas of Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad and Pune dominate the top 10.

## Data notes
- 148,410 restaurants across 821 distinct locations (areas and cities) analysed after cleaning.
- Rating counts on Swiggy are ranges (e.g. "1K+"), so they were converted to the lower bound.
- Pricing analysis only includes areas with 50+ restaurants, which removes outliers such as Hinganghat (avg cost 18,962 from very few listings).
